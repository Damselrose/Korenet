About KORENET

A technology partner that stays close after go-live.

KORENET is a Microsoft-focused consultancy built around a simple idea: the people who understand the problem should stay close to the solution.

Our approach

Configured around your process, not a template.

Off-the-shelf CRM and business-app templates assume every organisation works the same way. Real organisations do not. We start by understanding the process, the users, the exceptions and the outcomes that matter.

We then design and deliver a solution that can evolve. Because a CRM or business platform is never truly finished: processes change, teams change and Microsoft changes. Your technology partner should be able to change with you.

How we work with clients

The same five stages on every engagement.

Consistency creates clearer decisions, better estimates and fewer surprises.

Stage 01

Discover

Workshops with the people who actually use the process, not just the people who manage it.

Stage 02

Design

A written solution blueprint reviewed before build work starts.

Stage 03

Build

Short cycles with working screens shown early, so feedback happens before launch.

Stage 04

Deploy

Migration, training and a go-live plan timed around your business.

Stage 05

Support

Ongoing administration and enhancements from the team that understands the system.
